Ocoee homes were built in waves.

We do not know the condition of any particular house, but in a home from the 1980s or 1990s it is reasonable to ask a plumber about the age of the water heater, the supply shutoffs and any slab lines.

Plumbing emergency in Ocoee: what to do first.

If water is moving where it should not be, calling is the fastest way to reach a plumber. Do the first step on the list while you dial.

Pipe burst or leaking fast.

Close the main valve, then call. For a burst pipe, call right away about burst pipe repair.

Sewage backing up.

Stop running water and keep everyone out of the wet room. See sewer line repair for what happens next.

Water heater leaking.

Cut power or gas to the heater and close its cold inlet. Replacement is often the answer for a leaking tank.

Meter moving with taps off.

A slab or yard leak may be the cause, and leak detection finds it without tearing up the floor.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first.
