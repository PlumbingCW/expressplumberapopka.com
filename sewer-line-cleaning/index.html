When the main sewer line is blocked, every drain in the house is affected. Toilets bubble, tubs fill when the washer drains and sewage may surface at the lowest fixture. That is a sewer line cleaning emergency in Apopka.

The line is cleared with a heavy-duty cable or a jetter, and then a camera to confirm the pipe is open and sound. You see the result before we leave.

1

Sewer backup: what to do while we come.

Take these steps right away.

Stop the inflow.

Do not flush, run the washer or use sinks.

Open the cleanout cap carefully.

If safe, loosen the cleanout cap to release pressure, with gloves and eye protection.

Protect belongings.

Lift items off the affected floor and open windows for air.

Do not use drain chemicals.

They are ineffective on main lines and unsafe for the plumbers.

2

Why main lines clog.

The usual causes are grease that has cooled and hardened, wipes labeled flushable, roots entering at joints and a belly where solids collect. Each leaves a different pattern, and the camera shows which.

A clog that returns after a few months points to roots or a belly. A clog that appears suddenly after heavy use points to a foreign object or a mass of wipes.

3

Cable or jetter: how we decide.

A cable is quick and effective for soft clogs and roots in a basic line. Hydro jetting cleans the whole pipe wall and suits grease and scale.

We start with the least aggressive method that will work, and move to jetting when the first result does not hold. If the camera shows damage, we discuss repair rather than just cleaning.

5

Septic systems and city sewer.

A home on city sewer ties into a municipal main, while a home on septic has a tank and drain field. If you do not know which you have, check your utility bill or ask the City of Apopka. A full septic tank can mimic a sewer clog, so the plumber confirms the system before cleaning.

Prevention for Apopka homes.

Keep grease out of drains, flush only toilet paper and consider scheduled cleaning if your line is old. Homes near mature trees should watch for slow drains in the growing season.
