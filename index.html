1

What counts as a plumbing emergency.

Not every plumbing problem needs a midnight call, but these four do. Treat them as urgent and phone the line while you take the first step listed.

Burst pipe or fast leak.

Shut off the main water valve first, then call. Our burst pipe repair page explains what the plumber will do on arrival.

Sewage backing up.

Stop running water in the house and keep everyone away from the affected floor. See sewer line repair for how backups are traced.

Water heater leaking or no hot water.

Turn off the power or gas supply to the heater and close its cold water inlet. Compare repair against replacement when we talk.

Overflowing toilet or clogged main drain.

Close the toilet supply valve and stop using every drain. A plumber doing drain cleaning can often clear a line the same day, so ask about timing.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. Check your gas bill for the utility name, since in much of the Apopka area it is the Lake Apopka Natural Gas District.

Why Apopka pipes fail the way they do.

Apopka was chartered as a town in 1882, and electricity reached it on February 10, 1915, so the blocks around Main Street and 5th Street include some of the city's oldest homes. We do not know what pipe any particular house has, and no source we cite says what the older homes contain. If your house dates from the early or middle 1900s, ask a plumber to look at the pipe before you spend money on cosmetic repairs.

Newer communities such as Wekiva Club and Wekiva Crossing on the northeast side, and Lake Pleasant Cove and Country Address in the southeast, are a different story. Plumbing in newer houses fails at connections, valves and water heaters far more often than in the pipe itself, which is why a quick fixture check is usually the first step.

Weather matters too. Apopka averages about 50 inches of rain a year, and the record low is 15 degrees, so a hard freeze can crack an exposed hose bib or an uninsulated pipe on a cold night. Wrap exterior faucets before a forecast freeze and you avoid a call that happens at the worst hour.

What affects the price of a plumbing job.

Every job is different, so we do not post a menu of fixed prices. These are the real factors behind any quote you receive from us or anyone else.

The problem itself.
A cartridge swap and a collapsed drain line are very different jobs, even when both start with a drip.
Access.
A pipe in an open crawlspace costs less to reach than one behind tile or under a slab.
Parts and materials.
Water heater size, fuel type and pipe material move the total more than labor does.
Permits and inspection.
Some work needs a permit. Ask the City of Apopka building division whether yours does, because rules vary by job.
Time of day.
After-hours emergency work can price differently from a scheduled visit, and you hear that before work starts.

Homeowner insurance often covers sudden damage from a burst pipe but usually not the cost of repairing a worn pipe itself, so ask your carrier before you assume either way.
