Toilet Repair in Apopka, FL.

Toilet repair fixes a running, clogged, leaking or weak-flushing toilet by replacing worn parts or resealing the base. Most Apopka toilet calls are quick jobs, and an overflow is worth a call right away.

A toilet is simple, which is why small faults are easy to fix and easy to ignore. A silent running toilet can waste thousands of gallons a month, and a slow leak at the base can rot the subfloor for months before anyone notices.

We repair what can be repaired and say so when a toilet should be replaced. In Apopka, FL a toilet repair is usually done in a single visit.

1

Toilet running or flushing weakly.

Most running toilets have a worn flapper, a bad fill valve or a chain that is too long or too short. These parts are cheap, and swapping them takes less than an hour. A weak flush can come from clogged rim jets, a low water level in the tank or a partial clog in the drain.

To test for a silent leak, add a few drops of food coloring to the tank. If color shows in the bowl without flushing, the flapper is leaking.

3

Toilet overflowing: what to do now.

If water is rising, do these steps right away.

Lift the tank lid.

Push the flapper down to stop water entering the bowl.

Close the supply valve.

Turn the oval handle at the wall clockwise until it stops.

Stop other drains.

If more than one fixture backs up, the main line may be blocked. See sewer line cleaning.

Mop and ventilate.

Remove water quickly to protect the floor.

4

Leak at the base.

Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed, and it can also come from a loose closet bolt or a cracked bowl. Left alone, it seeps under flooring and can damage the subfloor.

The plumber lifts the toilet, checks the flange, fits a new seal and resets it level. If the flange is broken, they repair it before setting the toilet back.

5

Repeat clogs and plunger failures.

A toilet that clogs often may have a partial blockage further down. If a plunger does not clear it, a closet auger is the next tool, and if the problem returns the cause may be in the line. See drain cleaning for how those clogs are handled.

Wipes, paper towels and feminine products are the usual culprits. Even products marked flushable do not break down like toilet paper.

6

Repair or replace.

A toilet more than 20 years old that needs repeated repairs is often cheaper to replace. Newer models use less water per flush, and a replacement fits in one visit. We will tell you honestly if repair makes sense.
