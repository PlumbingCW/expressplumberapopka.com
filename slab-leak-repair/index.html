Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Apopka, FL.

A slab leak is a water line leaking in or under the concrete foundation of a home. Plumbers pinpoint it with listening gear, then repair or reroute the line.

Slab leaks hide. The first sign is often a water bill that climbs for no reason, a warm patch on a tile floor or the sound of running water when everything is off. By the time water shows at the surface, the line has usually been leaking for a while.

Many homes in Apopka, FL are built on concrete slabs, so supply lines and drain lines often run beneath the floor. We find the leak first and explain the repair options before anything is opened.

1

Suspected slab leak: what to do first.

Take these steps while you wait for the plumber.

Check the meter.

Turn everything off. If the meter keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere.

Close the main valve.

That stops the loss and protects the floors while you wait.

Feel the floors.

A warm spot on a hot water line can mark the leak location. Tell the person who answers where.

Move belongings.

Lift rugs and furniture off wet flooring.

2

Signs of a slab leak.

Common signs include a spinning meter with taps off, a hot spot on the floor, damp or buckling flooring, cracks in walls, a musty smell, low water pressure and the sound of running water at night. Any one of these is worth a call, and several together make a slab leak likely.

Our leak detection page explains how the search works, and slab work starts with that confirmation.

4

Hot water and cold water lines.

Hot water lines under a slab often fail first, because heat speeds wear on copper. A hot spot on the floor and a water heater that runs constantly can both point to a hot line leak. Cold lines and drains can also leak, and the plumber uses pressure tests to tell them apart.

5

What the damage looks like.

Water under a slab can undermine the soil and lift flooring. Moisture also encourages mold in the area. That is why quick detection matters, and why you should photograph the damage for your insurance claim.

If water is visible, our burst pipe repair steps apply as well.

Slab leaks in Apopka.

Slab foundations are common across Central Florida, and Apopka's roughly 50 inches of yearly rain keeps the ground damp. Older homes with copper under the slab are more prone to pinholes, while newer homes face leaks at fittings and joints. A pressure test tells which.
