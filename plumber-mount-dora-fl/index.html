A lakeside town with older lines.

Mount Dora was settled in the 1870s and was named for Lake Dora in 1883. The railroad arrived in 1887, and the city received its charter in 1910. By the 1920s the city was investing in a water system, streetlights and paved roads, so some of its oldest neighborhoods have had public utilities for a long time.
