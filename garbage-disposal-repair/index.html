A garbage disposal grinds food waste so it can pass through the drain. When it fails, the sink backs up, and a leak from the unit can soak the cabinet floor quickly.

We service most brands in Apopka, FL and give you an honest repair or replace call. Never put a hand into a disposal, and always switch off the power before anyone checks it.

Disposal jammed or humming: safe first steps.

Do this before you call.

Switch off the disposal.

Turn off the wall switch, and unplug the unit if you can reach the cord.

Never use your hand.

Use tongs or pliers to remove any visible object.

Use the hex key.

Insert the wrench into the bottom socket and work it back and forth to free the jam.

Press the reset button.

Wait a few minutes after a trip, then press the red button on the bottom.

2

Why disposals fail.

Jams are the most common cause, followed by overload and a burned motor. Bones, fruit pits, grease and stringy vegetables can stall the blades. A hum with no movement means the blades are stuck, while silence means a tripped breaker, a bad switch or a dead motor.

A leak is different. Water from the bottom is usually a failed seal, and water at the top is often a loose sink flange or a worn gasket.

4

Double sinks and shared drains.

Many kitchens have two sink bowls with one drain trap and a disposal on one side. Plumbing for a double sink with a disposal needs the right connection to the second bowl, or water from one side backs up into the other. If you see that, the cause is often a bad connection, not the disposal itself.

Our drain cleaning page covers kitchen line clogs that sit past the disposal.

5

What not to put down a disposal.

Grease, coffee grounds in large amounts, pasta, rice, celery and potato peels are all problem foods. Run cold water while it works and for a few seconds afterward. A clogged kitchen line can lead to a bigger backup, which is why habits matter.

6

Repair or replace.

A disposal over ten years old with a leak or burned motor is usually better replaced. A jam or a reset is a short repair. Replacement includes a new mounting assembly and a check of the drain connection.
