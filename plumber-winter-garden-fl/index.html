Water and sewer news in Winter Garden.

Winter Garden's city website lists current utility work. The Wastewater Treatment Facility at 101 E. Crest Avenue is being upgraded, with construction that began in mid-February 2025 and is expected to run about 36 months. The city has also posted that fire hydrant flow testing can briefly lower water pressure and discolor water.

Those notices matter to homeowners because a short pressure dip or cloudy water can look like a household problem. If water looks off, run a cold tap for a minute and check the city website before you assume a pipe has failed. If it persists, call us.

When a Winter Garden plumbing problem cannot wait.

Treat these as emergencies and call the line while you take the first step.

Burst pipe or major leak.

Close the main valve first. See burst pipe repair for what the plumbers does.

Sewer or drain backup.

Stop using water and keep clear of the affected area. Sewer line cleaning clears most main line backups.

No hot water.

Turn off the heater's power or gas, and check for leaks at the tank. Repair or replacement follows.

Overflowing toilet.

Close the supply valve at the wall and stop other drains.

A historic downtown and fast-growing neighborhoods.

Winter Garden was incorporated as a city in 1908, and its Plant Street downtown is a historic district. A remodel of an older home in the historic residential district is a good time to ask for a camera check on the drain.

South of SR 50, large communities were developed on former grove and farmland, and the city's population nearly doubled between 2000 and 2010. Those newer houses mostly fail at water heaters, valves and supply lines, not in the pipe itself.
