Houses from the 1970s boom.

Altamonte Springs was incorporated on November 11, 1920, but most of its housing arrived later. The city's population jumped from 4,391 in 1970 to 22,028 in 1980, and the 2020 census counted 46,231 people. That means many houses and condos here are now 40 to 55 years old.

Plumbing from that era has a pattern: original water heaters long since replaced, galvanized or early copper supply lines, cast iron drains that corrode from the inside and condo stacks that serve several units. A plumber who knows that pattern finds problems faster.
