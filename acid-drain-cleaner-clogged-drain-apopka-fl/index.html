Acid Drain Cleaner for a Clogged Drain: An Apopka, FL Guide.

Acid drain cleaners promise to dissolve almost any clog, and the bottle makes it look easy. The reality is that these products are among the most hazardous things sold for the home, and they often fail on the clogs people use them for.

This guide explains how they work, what can go wrong and what to do instead.

What acid drain cleaner is.

Most strong acid drain cleaners are concentrated sulfuric acid. They work by reacting with the organic material in a clog and generating heat. In a pipe full of standing water they can take a long time to reach the clog, and the acid can sit in the pipe and burn whatever it touches.

1

Safety warnings before you use any drain chemical.

Read this first.

Never mix products.

Mixing drain cleaners or other cleaners can release dangerous fumes.

Protect skin and eyes.

Splashes cause serious burns. Wear goggles and gloves.

Ventilate.

Open windows and run a fan.

Keep the plunger away.

Plunging a drain full of chemical can splash it back at you.

2

What it can do to your pipes.

Strong acid can damage metal pipe, soften some plastics and attack joints and fittings. If it does not clear the clog, the plumber who arrives next faces a pipe full of hazardous liquid, which is dangerous for them and often delays the repair. Be sure to tell the plumber you used a chemical.

3

Septic systems and acid.

Homes with a septic tank rely on bacteria to break down waste. Strong chemicals can disrupt that process and send harsh liquid into the tank. If your property is on septic, avoid drain chemicals and ask a plumber for a safer fix. See our Zellwood page for rural properties.

5

Why chemicals often do not work.

Clogs made of hair, wipes, grease or a solid object are not reliably dissolved by acid. Even when it clears a small opening, the rest of the buildup is still there, and the clog returns. A repeat clog is a sign that something is wrong with the pipe, not the cleaner.

6

When to stop and call.

Call a plumber if more than one fixture is slow, if the clog returns after a snake, if you smell sewage or if you have already used chemicals without success. For a main line problem, see sewer line cleaning and hydro jetting.

7

If you already poured it in.

Do not run water into the drain, do not plunge and do not add another product. Keep the area ventilated and keep children and pets away. Note the product name and the amount, then call a plumber and tell them before they begin. If chemical touches your skin or eyes, flush with water for at least 15 minutes and call poison control or seek medical care.

The plumber will wear protective gear and may need to neutralize or pump out the liquid before clearing the clog. Saying so up front protects the plumbers and speeds the repair.

8

Prevention beats chemicals.

Most household clogs are preventable. Use a hair catcher in the shower, keep grease out of kitchen drains, flush only toilet paper and run hot water for a few seconds after using the sink. A monthly flush with hot water and a gentle enzyme product keeps slow drains from turning into stoppages, and it costs far less than an emergency visit.

9

Reading the label.

Check the label for the active ingredient, the pipe types it is rated for and the warnings about septic systems. Many labels say not to use the product in a toilet or in a drain that is fully blocked. If the label confuses you, that is a signal to call a plumber instead of experimenting. Store any chemical out of reach of children in its original container.

Clogs and disposal in Apopka.

Apopka's website lists a solid waste and recycling page, which tells you how the city handles household waste. Pouring leftover drain chemical down a drain or throwing it out with the trash can be a hazard, so check with the City of Apopka about how to dispose of household hazardous products.

Many Apopka neighborhoods are on city sewer, and the city's utility pages explain service questions. Properties outside the city, or in nearby places such as Zellwood, may use septic tanks and should avoid strong chemicals.
