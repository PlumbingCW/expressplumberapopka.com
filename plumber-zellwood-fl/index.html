A drained-marsh history that still shapes the ground.

Zellwood was named for T. Ellwood Zell, who began wintering here in 1876, and its post office opened in 1877. In 1941 the state created the Zellwood Drainage and Water Control District to drain marshland north of Lake Apopka, which opened the land to farming. Sweet corn dominated from the 1950s through the 1980s, and the Zellwood Sweet Corn Festival ran from 1968 until 2013.

Drained marshland tends to stay low and damp, so a leaking buried line or an overflowing tank can show up as a soggy spot long before water appears in the house.

Landmarks and community around Zellwood.
